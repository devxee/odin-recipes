Pani Puri recipe

Description: We can call pani puri as water balls in english. The smashed potatoes are filled inside the puris, we then dip the puri in masala water, sprinkle roasted lentils and bhujia on top of it and engulf. lol

Ingredients List

  • Spicy sour water with all masala
  • Puri
  • Smashed boiled potatoes
  • Roasted lentils and bhujia
